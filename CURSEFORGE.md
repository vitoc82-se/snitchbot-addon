# Snitchbot

**Know how your runs actually went.** Snitchbot tracks every dungeon run, your leveling pace and your gold for you. It also tells you what to bring on raid night. It's all in one window and starts by itself, so there are no commands to remember.

Made for **WoW Forever**, and part of [Snitchbot](https://snitchbot.app): the free guild planner, auction house and character pages for WoW Forever.

## Dungeon runs, tracked by themselves

Walk into a dungeon and Snitchbot starts a run. You get:

- **Time, kills, XP and XP per hour**
- **Bosses:** every pull, with kills, wipes and how long each fight took
- **Loot:** green and better items, who got them and which boss dropped them
- **Gold**, plus what **your loot is worth** at auction house or vendor prices
- **Reputation** gained, deaths, and **who you ran with**

Wipes, corpse runs and waiting at the door all stay part of the same run. When a run ends, Snitchbot compares it with your earlier runs of that dungeon: *"New best for The Deadmines: 35m (was 40m)."* There's also an **End run** button if you want the summary right away.

## Personal bests and stats

The **Stats** tab collects it all:

- **Personal bests** for each dungeon: fastest full clear and best XP per hour
- **Time played per level**, and roughly when you'll reach the next milestone at your pace
- **Where your XP comes from:** kills, quests and exploring
- **Where your gold comes from:** loot, quests, vendors, the auction house, trades, repairs, training and flights
- **Boss loot:** what each boss has dropped across your runs
- **Who you run with** most

## Raid night

- **Raid list:** paste your guild's raid shopping list from [auction.snitchbot.app](https://auction.snitchbot.app). Snitchbot counts what's in your bags and bank and shows what's still missing.
- **Check buffs:** one click lists who in your group is missing a flask, an elixir or food, and how many have world buffs.
- **Repair warning:** if your gear is low on durability when a dungeon starts, you hear about it before the pull.

## Your stats on the web

Press **Copy for snitchbot.app** and paste it on [myself.snitchbot.app](https://myself.snitchbot.app). Your character's page then shows your runs, personal bests, time per level and where your gold came from. Only you can see it.

To value loot at real auction prices, press **Copy prices for the addon** on [auction.snitchbot.app](https://auction.snitchbot.app) and paste it into Snitchbot (**Settings → Paste AH prices**).

## Good to know

- **Combat log:** Snitchbot can start the game's combat log when you enter a dungeon and stop it when you leave. You can turn this off in Settings.
- **Group message:** when a run starts in a group, Snitchbot says once in group chat that it's tracking the run. You can turn this off in Settings → Tell my group. Nothing is said when you're solo.
- **Your data stays with you:** everything is saved in your own SavedVariables. Nothing leaves the game unless you copy it yourself.
- **Commands are optional:** use the minimap button or `/sb`. Shortcuts include `/sb stats`, `/sb check` and `/sb export`.
